Legal & Booking Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Please review our professional service agreements and booking terms for travel across London, Bath, and the UK.

1. Booking & Confirmation

All chauffeur reservations must be made via our online booking console, direct email, or authorized telephone/WhatsApp dispatch. Bookings are subject to vehicle availability and written/SMS confirmation.

2. Airport Transfers & Waiting Times

We provide 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time for airport arrivals (Heathrow, Gatwick, etc.) from the actual flight landing time, alongside live flight tracking. Standard pickups include 15 minutes of free waiting time.

3. Cancellations & Refund Policy

Cancellations made at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled pickup time are eligible for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a late cancellation fee covering driver deployment costs.

4. Payment Terms

Payments can be settled securely via approved online methods or direct corporate agreements. Fixed rates quoted at the time of booking cover standard mileage, tolls, and congestion charges unless specified otherwise.

5. Conduct & Safety

Our chauffeurs maintain the right to refuse service to any passenger exhibiting unsafe, abusive, or unlawful behavior. Smoking, consumption of illegal substances, and open alcohol containers are strictly prohibited in all executive vehicles.

6. Liability

Royal Chauffeur London & Bath maintains full commercial vehicle insurance. However, we are not held liable for delays caused by severe traffic accidents, extreme weather conditions, road closures, or acts of God beyond our operational control.
