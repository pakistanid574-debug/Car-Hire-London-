Legal & Booking Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Please review our professional service agreements and Privacy Policy for travel across London, Bath, and the UK.

1. Booking & Confirmation

All chauffeur reservations must be made via our online booking console, WhatsApp concierge, or direct email dispatch. Bookings are subject to vehicle availability and written confirmation.

2. Airport Transfers & Waiting Times

We provide 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time for airport arrivals (Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Farnborough) from the actual flight landing time, alongside live flight tracking. Standard point-to-point pickups include 15 minutes of free waiting time.

3. Cancellations & Refund Policy

Cancellations made at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled pickup time are eligible for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a late cancellation fee covering driver deployment costs.

4. Payment Terms

Payments can be settled securely via approved online methods, major credit/debit cards, or direct corporate invoicing. Fixed rates quoted at the time of booking cover standard mileage, tolls, and London Congestion Charges unless specified otherwise.

5. Conduct & Safety

Our chauffeurs maintain the right to refuse service to any passenger exhibiting unsafe, abusive, or unlawful behavior. Smoking, consumption of illegal substances, and open alcohol containers are strictly prohibited in all executive vehicles.

6. Liability, Licensing & Data Privacy

Royal Chauffeur London & Bath operates with licensed private hire drivers and full commercial fleet insurance, though we are not held liable for delays caused by severe traffic accidents, extreme weather, road closures, or circumstances beyond our operational control. For details on how we handle booking data and cookies, please read our full Privacy Policy.
