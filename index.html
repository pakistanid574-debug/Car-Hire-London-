Service Terms

Terms & Conditions

Review our booking terms and Privacy Policy before confirming travel.

1. Booking & Confirmation

Chauffeur reservations are subject to vehicle availability and written confirmation following your online or WhatsApp quote request.

2. Airport Transfers & Waiting Times

Airport arrivals at Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Farnborough, and Birmingham (BHX) include flight tracking and up to 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time from actual landing, subject to your booking confirmation. Standard pickups include 15 minutes of waiting time.

3. Cancellations & Changes

Schedule updates or cancellations made at least 24 hours before pickup are handled per your confirmed booking terms. Late cancellations within 24 hours may incur a charge.

4. Pricing & Charges

Quoted prices apply to the agreed route, vehicle class, and schedule. Additional stops, waiting beyond grace periods, tolls, or congestion charges will be stated clearly where applicable.

5. Passenger Safety & Conduct

Drivers may decline or end a journey if passenger behavior poses a safety risk or violates UK law. Smoking and illegal substances are not permitted inside vehicles.

6. Liability & Privacy

Services operate under commercial insurance and applicable UK regulations, though we are not responsible for delays caused by traffic incidents, weather, road closures, or events outside our control. See our Privacy Policy for data handling details.
