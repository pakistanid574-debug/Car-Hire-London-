Planning Your Journey to Royal Hospital Chelsea

The Chelsea Flower Show takes place at Royal Hospital Chelsea, located in the Chelsea area of London. Visitors should check the official RHS website for the latest event dates, admission details, entrance information and visitor guidance.

Before travelling, consider your preferred arrival time, collection address and return arrangements. Event traffic and temporary road restrictions may affect journey times, so allow additional time when planning your visit.

For official event information, visit the RHS Chelsea Flower Show website . You can also check Transport for London for public transport and travel updates.
