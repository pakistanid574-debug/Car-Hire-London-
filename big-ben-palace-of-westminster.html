Big Ben & Palace of Westminster

📍 London, England

Big Ben is the nickname for the Great Bell of the striking clock at the north end of the Palace of Westminster in London. Towering over the River Thames, this iconic landmark represents the political heart of the United Kingdom and is one of London's most recognizable architectural marvels, especially beautiful when illuminated at night.
