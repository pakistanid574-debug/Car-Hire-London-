2027 Parade Route & How It Affects Central London Traffic

The parade covers roughly 2.2 miles (3.5 km) through Central London:

  1. Start — Piccadilly (near Green Park): Procession sets off from the Green Park end of Piccadilly heading towards Piccadilly Circus.
  2. Mid-Route — Regent Street St James's & Pall Mall: Moves south through St James's and east along Pall Mall towards Trafalgar Square.
  3. Finish — Trafalgar Square, Whitehall & Parliament Square: Continues past Trafalgar Square and Whitehall towards Westminster/Parliament Square.

Because roads along this corridor experience full or rolling closures and surrounding streets see heavy pedestrian and traffic management, standard app rideshares frequently cancel or drop passengers far from their hotels. A pre-arranged chauffeur coordinates practical pickup points just outside the restricted perimeter.
