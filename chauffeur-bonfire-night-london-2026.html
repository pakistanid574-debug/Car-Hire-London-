What Is Bonfire Night?

Bonfire Night, also known as Guy Fawkes Night, is traditionally observed across the United Kingdom on 5 November. The occasion commemorates the historical Gunpowder Plot of 1605 and has developed into a night of fireworks and community celebrations.

Modern Bonfire Night celebrations often include fireworks displays, food stalls, family activities and organised public events. In London, different venues and communities may host their own celebrations.

Bonfire Night London 2026: Planning Your Evening

Planning transportation is an important part of attending a busy evening event. London roads and public spaces can become crowded around popular fireworks locations.

Before travelling, visitors should check the latest event information, admission requirements and transportation updates from the relevant event organiser.

  • Check the official event date and opening times.
  • Confirm whether tickets or advance booking are required.
  • Review the event's location and entry arrangements.
  • Check road closures and local traffic restrictions.
  • Plan your return journey before the event begins.
  • Allow extra time for busy evening traffic.
Travel tip: Fireworks events can attract large crowds. Check official organiser announcements and local transport updates before setting off.

London Bonfire Night Transportation Tips

London event traffic can be unpredictable, particularly around busy venues and central locations. The following suggestions can help visitors prepare.

Check Pickup Locations Carefully

Temporary road restrictions may affect access to event entrances and nearby streets. Confirm a suitable pickup and drop-off location before travelling.

Allow Additional Travel Time

Traffic congestion, pedestrian crowds and road closures can affect journey times. Avoid scheduling tight connections around major events.

Plan Your Return Journey

After fireworks displays finish, many attendees leave at similar times. Consider your return transportation arrangements before arriving at the event.

Keep Event Information Available

Save your event ticket, venue address and transportation details on your phone. This can help when navigating busy locations.
