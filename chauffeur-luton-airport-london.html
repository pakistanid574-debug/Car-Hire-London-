How Meet & Greet Works at London Luton Airport

Luton Airport operates a single main passenger terminal, which keeps arrivals straightforward—though the forecourt drop-off and pickup zones have strict time limits and barrier fees. With our Meet & Greet service, you never have to walk to an off-site shuttle bus stop.

  1. Flight Monitoring: Using your flight number, we track your actual landing time at LTN.
  2. Terminal Arrival Greeting: Your chauffeur parks in the Luton Short Stay Car Park and waits inside the Arrivals concourse holding a clear name board.
  3. Luggage Assistance: Your driver assists with your bags and escorts you directly to the waiting vehicle just a short walk from the terminal exit.
  4. Direct Door-to-Door Transfer: Relax in climate-controlled comfort straight to your London hotel, office, or residence.
